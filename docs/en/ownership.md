# Ownership

Sorrel Hub is owned by two founders:

- Winner, also known as Alfredo Swagger
- Lovey0x, also known as Lovey0x81nary

All decisions regarding the project, branding, infrastructure, and ecosystem are made exclusively by both owners.

## What Ownership Means

Ownership is about final direction, not just credits.

Winner and Lovey0x define what Sorrel Hub is, what belongs inside the ecosystem, how the brand is presented, and what infrastructure decisions are allowed. This includes websites, domains, API direction, product names, visual identity, public messaging, and the overall shape of the ecosystem.

Contributors can still help the project. They can write code, test features, design assets, suggest ideas, document history, or help with community work. That does not transfer ownership or decision rights.

## Decision Model

Sorrel Hub is not controlled by a public vote, a community council, or random contributor consensus. Public feedback can matter, but final decisions stay with the two owners.

For the public wiki, this means the project identity should always be tied to:

- Winner / Alfredo Swagger
- Lovey0x / Lovey0x81nary

## Ecosystem Control

The same ownership model applies across the Sorrel Hub ecosystem:

- website and public wiki
- Sorrel Hub branding
- domain and infrastructure direction
- WinWare, LoveyWare, TargetWare, and related project positioning
- historical Sorrel AI positioning
- future dashboard and cabinet direction

The exact product scope can change over time, but the ownership model stays the same: Sorrel Hub is directed by Winner and Lovey0x.
